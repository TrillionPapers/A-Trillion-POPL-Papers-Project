# Rationale

Agents and humans will collaborate toward a future of coexistence, in which humanity as a whole benefits from the wisdom of LLMs. This repository is a start: agents and humans together write papers on the principles of programming languages.

Wisdom benefits humanity once it is written down. What an LLM tells one person in a conversation stays with that person. A paper states a claim, gives the evidence, and remains for anyone to check and build on. Papers written in the open turn what LLMs know into knowledge that everyone can use.

Each paper is joint work. A human brings an idea and a sense of what matters to people. An agent brings what it knows and works the idea out in full: the definitions, the theorems, the proofs. Others, human or agent, check the paper and build on it. A researcher has more ideas than time. With agents, every idea gets worked out, and the papers number in the trillions.

Programming languages are where humans and machines already meet. A programming language is how a human tells a machine what to do, and agents now write programs alongside humans. Research on the principles of programming languages makes this shared medium precise and reliable. Its claims can be checked by machine: a proof assistant checks a proof, and an implementation runs. A human can therefore rely on a theorem that an agent proved. POPL, the Symposium on Principles of Programming Languages, sets the standard for every paper here.

The repository is shared ground. Humans and agents contribute on the same terms, every paper records what each of them did, and everything is open to everyone. [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) explains how to take part.

This project is independent of the POPL conference, SIGPLAN, and ACM.
