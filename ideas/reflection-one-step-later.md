# Reflection, one step later

A system that can speak about its own proofs cannot prove, for every P, that P holds if P is provable. By Löb's theorem it would then prove everything. A proof checker that wants to install a successor it has verified meets this limit, and the literature on agents that rewrite themselves calls it the Löbian obstacle ([Yudkowsky and Herreshoff, 2013](https://intelligence.org/files/TilingAgentsDraft.pdf)). Guarded type theory has a later modality with a Löb rule of its own, which is induction on computation steps. The idea is the principle "if P is provable, then later P", for the provability of the very system that contains the principle. It is consistent by induction on stages in the topos of trees: the axiom holds at stage n because the system is sound at stage n - 1. With it a checker may install a new kernel that comes with a proof that the kernel is sound later, and "safe at every step" follows by Löb induction. A paper would give the principle in a guarded dependent type theory with an internal syntax, its model, a calculus of programs that replace themselves under proof together with the theorem that such programs stay safe, and the limit of the method, which is safety properties. It would also settle how the principle relates to universe levels: an interpreter of a theory needs one level more than the theory, and by [paper 23](https://trillionpapers.github.io/A-Trillion-POPL-Papers-Project/p/23/) a later can pay for one level.

What we know on 22 November 2026. The work on the obstacle treats Löb's theorem as the obstruction and has a way around it with a parameter that counts down, and a version of the theorem for bounded proofs ([Critch, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2017.42)). The work on guarded recursion uses Löb as a proof rule. We found nothing that joins the two. The doubt is that the counting parameter is the step index under another name. Then the idea restates a known trick, and it is worth a paper only if the type theory gives something that the arithmetic does not, such as self-replacement that carries proofs for programs with state.
