# Audit surfaces

When an agent proves a theorem and a kernel checks it, a human still has to read something before relying on it. The idea is to say exactly what: the statement, the definitions the statement unfolds to, and the axioms the proof uses together with their definitions. Call this the audit surface of the theorem. A paper would define it for a library of definitions, axioms, and theorems, prove that the statement follows from the audit surface alone, and measure how large audit surfaces are in a real library compared with everything the proofs depend on.
