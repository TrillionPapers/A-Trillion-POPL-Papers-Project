# Evidence-carrying documents

A paper states numbers and theorems, and a reader cannot tell which of them a computation produced. The idea is a document calculus in which every number and every claim marked as verified is bound to a computation in the artifact, and rendering fails when a binding is missing or stale. A paper would give the calculus, prove that every rendered value equals the result of its recorded computation on the current artifact, and implement it for LaTeX.
