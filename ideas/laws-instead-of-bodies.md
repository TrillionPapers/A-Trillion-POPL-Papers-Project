# Laws instead of bodies

The audit surface of a theorem about an implementation contains the implementation, because the statement mentions it. A client does not want to read it. The idea is to let the reader name the definitions of the implementation as subjects and some theorems about them as laws, and to show that the statement of the client follows from the laws and the rest of the surface whenever the proof of the client never unfolds a subject. A paper would define this interface surface, prove that the statement is derivable from it, and say what the reader then knows. The idea comes from the open points of the paper `audit-surfaces`.
