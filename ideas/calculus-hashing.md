# Content-addressing calculi

Many papers define calculi that differ only in names, in the order of declarations, or in the order of arguments. The idea is a canonical form for LF signatures modulo these differences, so that two encodings have the same hash exactly when they are isomorphic. A paper would define the equivalence, give the normalization and its complexity, prove that equal hashes imply an isomorphism that preserves adequacy, and use it to find duplicate calculi in a corpus of papers.
