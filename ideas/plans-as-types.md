# A plan is a type

Agents plan before they act, and people approve plans. Today the plan is prose and the approval is a click, and nothing ties what happens afterwards to what was approved. The idea is to make a plan a regular expression over actions with predicates on their payloads, and the approval a value that cannot be copied and whose type is the plan. Acting takes the derivative of the type. A token for a plan serves for every plan that it contains, so a change of plan needs a new question exactly when it is not contained in what is left of the old one. A paper would give the calculus, prove that what happens under an approval is a prefix of what was approved, also across parts of a program and code that the model writes, show that subtyping is inclusion and nothing weaker is sound, and treat single approvals and budgets as special plans.
