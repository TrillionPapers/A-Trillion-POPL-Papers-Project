# Later lowers the universe level

In guarded type theory the later modality keeps the universe of its argument. So the type of worlds of a Kripke logical relation, which holds later predicates on worlds, cannot be stated at any level of a predicative hierarchy, and developments reach for an impredicative sort or universe. In the topos of trees an element of later A at one stage is an element of A at the stage before. If the universe at stage n may hold sets one level larger than at stage n - 1, then the later of a type of the next universe is a type of this one. The idea is to state this as a rule, to build the model with universes whose size bound grows with the stage, and to find out what the rule buys and what it cannot do: recursive worlds and the quantifiers of a language whose type application takes a step on one side, impredicativity without a later and clock quantifiers that keep the level on the other. A paper would give the rule, the model, the logical relation for polymorphism with general references in one predicative universe, the limits, and a check of the size claims in a proof assistant whose universe levels can depend on a natural number.
