So let the prover say it.

A proof becomes three things: a term, some advice, and a price.

The advice is a string of letters, one for each question that the checker would ask: left, right, both, or compare the arguments.

For our equation, two letters are enough: unfold the right side, then compare the arguments.

The price is a number of steps. The checker counts, and stops when the price is used up.
